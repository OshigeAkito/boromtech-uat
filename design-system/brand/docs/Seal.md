The BoromTech seal, the company icon: a thick ring that is never filled, broken, stretched or recoloured outside its four tones.

- `<Seal tone="orange" />` on `night` and `paper`; `tone="rust"` on `soft` (orange is 2.7:1 there); `tone="ink"` on an orange fill; `tone="light"` on dark photographs.
- Keep 15% of its size clear on every side. Minimum 16px; below 24px it draws the heavier small-size ring by itself.
- One seal per view: don't place a `Seal` or `Lockup` next to a `HeroSeal`.

```jsx
<Seal tone="orange" size={48} title="BoromTech" />
```
