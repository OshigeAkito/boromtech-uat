The app icon and favicon: the small-size seal on a night square.

- Use it for browser tabs, home-screen and store icons, and avatar slots for the company account.
- The artwork is square; `masked` only previews the platform's rounded corners. Never add letters or swap the night square for orange.

```jsx
<AppIcon size={48} masked title="BoromTech" />
```
