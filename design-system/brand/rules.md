# Do and don't

## Do

- Use the wordmark alone as the logo: Warm Ivory on Deep Navy, Deep Navy on light grounds.
- Keep at least the height of the B clear around the wordmark.
- Put covers and section dividers on Deep Navy; put diagrams and detailed content on light grounds.
- Crop the big ring off the right edge of a cover, hero, card's brand side or post, as the website does.
- Spend orange only on what must be seen first: the ring, the one primary action, step numbers.
- Use `rust` for orange text on light grounds, and for a small ring on `soft` or `ivory`.
- Set text on orange fills in `ink`.
- Set Latin in Bricolage Grotesque (headings 700, body 400 to 600) and Thai in IBM Plex Sans Thai (headings 600 to 700, body 400).
- Keep corners square. Let the ring be the only curve.
- Give Thai and Chinese headlines their own leading (`hero-th`, `hero-zh`) and 0 tracking.
- Name the document, the ID and the date in every document header: "BoromTech · Audit Report · BT-A-0412".
- Pair every status colour with a word: "Critical 2", not a red dot.
- In presentations, let the presenter advance the slides and give Thai text time to be read.

## Don't

- Don't set orange text, or a small ring that stands for the company, on `soft` (2.7:1) or `ivory` (2.8:1). Use `rust`. Only the big cropped ring and hairlines may stay orange there.
- Don't set white or `on-dark` text on orange (2.8:1), or orange body text on white (3.2:1).
- Don't put the ring over text, and don't put letters, icons or photos inside it.
- Don't change the ring's thickness, fill it into a disc, break it into an arc or squash it into an oval.
- Don't add gradients, glows, bevels or drop shadows to the ring or the wordmark.
- Don't stretch, condense or re-proportion the wordmark or either typeface.
- Don't crop the ring from the left edge, or cut away more than half of it.
- Don't show more than one ring per view, and don't use the lockup in new work.
- Don't round the corners of buttons, cards or images.
- Don't set the wordmark in any face but the outlined Bricolage files, and don't recolour it orange.
- Don't track Thai text tighter than 0 or set it in uppercase-style eyebrows.
